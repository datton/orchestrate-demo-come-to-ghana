Coming home to Ghana

Moving back, or moving for the first time? Here is what is clear, what is not, and who to ask, so you never rely on a guess.

General information, not legal, tax or immigration advice. Confirm with the authority or a licensed Ghanaian adviser.

The route

Step by step

  1. Work out which status applies to you

    Ghana recognises dual nationality. A dual nationality card shows that no visa is needed. Check with the Ghana mission before relying on it.

    If you hold a foreign passport and are not a Ghanaian citizen, the visa rules for your passport apply, as in our tourist guide.

    Official sources: UK travel advice (entry) · E-visa portal

  2. Confirm your route with the Ghana mission

    A residence permit and any right-of-abode route could not be confirmed from an official source on 18.11.2026. We do not state rules we cannot verify: ask the Ghana mission in your country, or a licensed Ghanaian adviser, which route fits you.

    Official sources: Immigration Service

  3. Get the Non-Citizen Ghana Card if it applies

    Since 02.02.2026 foreigners aged 6 and over who are resident 90 days or more a year must hold a Non-Citizen Ghana Card. It applies to foreign passport holders without Ghanaian citizenship.

    Ghanaian citizens use the standard Ghana Card. Our guidance does not cover that process: check with the issuing authority.

    Official sources: Registration portal

  4. Sort your health paperwork before you fly

    Complete the online Health Declaration (mandatory since 12.11.2026). Bring a yellow fever certificate, valid for life, vaccinated at least 10 days before travel. See a travel clinic about malaria prophylaxis.

    Official sources: Health Declaration · TravelHealthPro

  5. Plan your shipping and money

    Duties on household goods and shipping could not be verified, so we give no figures. Ask a licensed customs agent and check the customs authority before you ship.

    Exchange money only at banks or licensed bureaus. If you carry more than US$10,000, declare it on form FX-5.

    Official sources: Customs (GRA) · Bank of Ghana

Before you go

What to prepare

  • Passport and, if you hold it, your dual nationality card
  • Proof of your citizenship or residence status for the Ghana mission
  • Photo under 3 months old and a return ticket or itinerary
  • Yellow fever certificate and the completed Health Declaration
  • A list of questions for the Ghana mission: residence, shipping, right of abode
  • Quotes from a licensed customs agent if shipping household goods
