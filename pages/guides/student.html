Studying in Ghana

Student visas follow a different route from the online e-visa. Here is what we can confirm, what to prepare, and where to check the rest.

Facts as of 18.11.2026

General information, not legal, tax or immigration advice. Confirm with the authority or a licensed Ghanaian adviser.

The route for students

Student visas are outside the e-visa. The online e-visa covers business and tourism only (EY, 06.2026), so the embassy route applies. We have not confirmed the student visa fees and document list: ask the Ghana mission for your country and your institution's admissions office. As of 18.11.2026.

Step by step

  1. Secure your admission letter

    Your institution's admission letter is the starting point for the student visa. Keep the original and a clear scan.

  2. Ask the Ghana mission for the current checklist

    Fees and documents for student visas have not been confirmed by us. The mission for your country sets the requirements and the application route.

  3. Prepare your passport and health items

    Passport valid 6 months or more beyond departure, a yellow fever certificate, and your malaria plan. Details below.

  4. Complete the Health Declaration before you fly

    Online and mandatory since 12.11.2026: ehds.ghs.gov.gh/declaration.

  5. Register for the Non-Citizen Ghana Card if you stay

    Compulsory for foreigners aged 6 and over who are resident 90 days or more a year, at fims.org.gh.

What to prepare

  • Passport valid for 6 months or more (apply the stricter rule: beyond your departure date).
  • Admission letter from your institution.
  • Any further documents the Ghana mission lists for your route (we have not confirmed the student list).
  • Yellow fever certificate for all arrivals aged 9 months and over: valid for life, vaccinate at least 10 days before travel. Source: CDC; TravelHealthPro (28.10.2026).
  • Online Health Declaration, mandatory since 12.11.2026, completed before departure: ehds.ghs.gov.gh/declaration.
  • Malaria risk across Ghana: ask a travel clinic about prophylaxis.

Arriving and settling in

  • Arrival airport: Accra International Airport (ACC), formerly Kotoka, renamed 23.02.2026.
  • Currency: the cedi (GHS). Exchange only at banks or licensed bureaus. About GH¢11.64 per US$ interbank on 09.11.2026 (indicative).
  • Power: 230 V, 50 Hz, Type G and D sockets. Outages occurred in 2026, so bring a power bank.
  • Emergency numbers: 112; police 191; ambulance 193; fire 192.
  • Safety: Canada advises a high degree of caution and no non-essential travel within 25 km of the Burkina Faso border. The UK advises against all but essential travel to Bawku Municipal. Same-sex acts are illegal in Ghana, and camouflage clothing is illegal.
  • Yellow fever outbreak in the Upper West Region (CDC notice 09.11.2026): avoid the area.
