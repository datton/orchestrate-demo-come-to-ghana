Working remotely from Ghana: what is clear and what is not

We found no dedicated Ghana remote-work visa, and we will not claim one exists. This guide sets out what the rules say, where the grey areas are and who to ask.

Step by step

  1. Know what is not confirmed

    No dedicated remote-work or digital nomad visa was found in our research as of 18.11.2026. Check with the Ghana Immigration Service before you plan around one.

  2. Choose your entry route

    Tourism and business entry run through the e-visa at evisa.immigration.gov.gh (US$260 standard single entry; stay up to 60 days, set at the border). Working for a foreign employer from Ghana on a tourist or business visa is a grey area, and working without a permit is prohibited, according to the e-visa portal. Ask a licensed immigration adviser.

  3. Prepare health and entry paperwork

    Complete the online Health Declaration before departure (mandatory since 12.11.2026) and carry your yellow fever certificate. See a travel clinic about malaria: risk is present across Ghana.

  4. Understand tax residence

    You are resident if you spend 183 or more days in any 12 months. Residents are taxed on worldwide income. Speak to a tax adviser in your home country and in Ghana.

  5. If you plan a longer stay

    A residence permit route and the Non-Citizen Ghana Card apply as for business travellers: the card is compulsory for foreigners aged 6 or more resident 90 or more days a year (US$120 first registration, US$78 a year to renew).

  6. Set up for work

    Plan for power cuts: outages occurred in 2026, so bring a UPS or power bank. Sockets are Type G and D at 230 V, 50 Hz. 3G and 4G coverage is strongest in cities. Mobile money rules for foreigners are not confirmed: ask your provider. The e-levy was abolished in 2025.

What to prepare

  • Passport valid 6+ months beyond departure, and a photo under 3 months old
  • Return ticket or itinerary, proof of accommodation and your yellow fever certificate
  • Written advice from a licensed immigration adviser on whether your work arrangement is allowed on your visa
  • A UPS or power bank, and a plan for work during outages
  • A copy of your employer's or clients' details, kept for any adviser or tax conversation
