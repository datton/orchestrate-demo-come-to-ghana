How it works

Four steps. No waiting.

No account to set up and no wait for a reply. Pay, and what you bought is ready at once.

From question to guide

  1. Choose your plan

    Read the guide for your traveller type, then pick the plan that matches. Each plan lists exactly what is included and its price in euro. Our service fee is separate from government fees, which are paid to the authority.

  2. Pay with a test card

    This is a demonstration site, so payment runs in test mode and no real money moves. Use the card number below with any future date and any three digits.

  3. Get your guide

    We offer guidance and document preparation, and we never promise approval: the authority decides. Your order is ready the moment payment goes through, with what you bought, what to prepare and what happens next.

  4. Find it again, and ask

    Every purchase stays under My purchases in this browser, with your guide unlocked. The order confirmation says how to reach us with questions about your plan.
