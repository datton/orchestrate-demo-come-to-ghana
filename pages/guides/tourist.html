Visiting Ghana as a tourist

Apply for the e-visa, sort your health paperwork, land at Accra and stay safe: what to do, in order, with the dated facts and where each comes from.

General information, not legal, tax or immigration advice. Confirm with the authority or a licensed Ghanaian adviser.

Step by step

  1. Check whether you need a visa

    The e-visa went online on 25.05.2026 for business and tourism. It is optional for non-Africans and mandatory for Africans who need a visa. Ordinary passports from Canada, the UK, all EU states, China, India, Japan, South Korea, the Philippines, Vietnam, Malaysia, Pakistan and Bangladesh need a visa. ECOWAS and AES states are visa-free. Singapore and visa-free Caribbean states get a free ETA.

    Visa on arrival was replaced by the May 2026 reform. Do not rely on it: you risk being denied boarding.

    Official sources: GIS visa requirements · E-visa portal

  2. Apply for the e-visa

    Apply online. You cannot edit an application after submitting, so check every answer first. Answer any request for information within 72 hours.

    Sources differ on processing time (48 to 96 hours). Allow a week or more.

    Official sources: E-visa portal

  3. Complete the Health Declaration

    The online Health Declaration is mandatory since 12.11.2026 and must be done before you leave.

    A yellow fever certificate is required for all arrivals aged 9 months and over. It is valid for life; vaccinate at least 10 days before travel. There is a yellow fever outbreak notice for the Upper West Region (CDC, 09.11.2026): avoid it. Malaria risk applies across Ghana: ask a travel clinic about prophylaxis.

    Official sources: Health Declaration · CDC traveler Ghana · TravelHealthPro

  4. Pack the documents

    Passport valid for 6 months or more (apply the stricter rule: beyond your departure date), a photo under 3 months old, a return ticket or itinerary, accommodation proof and your yellow fever certificate.

    Official sources: GIS requirements

  5. Arrive and get through the airport

    Accra International Airport (ACC), formerly Kotoka, was renamed on 23.02.2026. Expect thermal screening on arrival. Approval does not guarantee entry: your stay, up to 60 days, is set at the border, and you must enter within 90 days of the visa being issued.

    If you carry more than US$10,000 in cash, declare it on form FX-5. Duty-free allowance: spirits 375 ml, wine 750 ml, tobacco 0.5 kg.

    Official sources: E-visa portal · Customs (GRA)

  6. Stay safe and sensible

    Use licensed taxis, Uber or Bolt, and agree the fare first. Beaches can have rip tides. Use cash where practical, as card fraud occurs. Exchange money only at banks or licensed bureaus. Camouflage clothing is illegal and same-sex acts are illegal.

    Canada advises a high degree of caution and avoiding non-essential travel within 25 km of the Burkina Faso border. The UK advises against all but essential travel to the Bawku Municipal area. Emergency: 112; police 191; ambulance 193; fire 192.

    Official sources: Government of Canada · UK travel advice

What to prepare

  • Passport valid 6+ months beyond departure
  • Recent photo (under 3 months old)
  • Return ticket or itinerary and accommodation proof
  • Yellow fever certificate, vaccinated 10+ days before travel
  • Completed online Health Declaration
  • Power bank and a Type G or D plug adaptor (230 V, 50 Hz); outages occurred in 2026
  • Cash in small notes and a plan for licensed exchange only
