Common questions

Do you guarantee my visa?

No. The authority decides. We help you prepare a correct, complete application and tell you what to expect.

Is your fee the visa fee?

No. Our fee is for guidance and document preparation. Government fees, such as the e-visa, are set by the authority and paid separately.

Can I still get a visa on arrival?

The May 2026 reform replaced it. Do not rely on it, as you risk being denied boarding. Check the e-visa portal for your passport.

How long should I allow for an e-visa?

Sources differ, from 48 hours to 96 hours. We suggest allowing a week or more, and answering any request for information within 72 hours.

Are you a law firm or immigration practitioner?

No. We offer general information and document preparation. For land, company or tax questions, speak to a licensed Ghanaian adviser; our plans say where.

Is this a real shop?

It is a demonstration. Payments are test payments only and no real money moves.
