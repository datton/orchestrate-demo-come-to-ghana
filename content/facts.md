## 2. Plans by traveller type

### 2.1 Tourist
- Visa: e-visa tourist, US$260 (non-African); embassy route possible (fees UNCONFIRMED).
- Docs: passport, itinerary, accommodation proof, yellow fever certificate. Invitation letter not required for tourism (UNCONFIRMED: the portal checklist was not final at launch).
- Stay: up to 60 days. Extension in person at GIS, Independence Avenue, Accra: GH¢65 per month. Overstay: GH¢300 per month (GIS fees, effective 15.05.2025).
- Safety, taxis (licensed taxis, Uber, Bolt; agree fare first), beaches (rip tides), card fraud: use cash where practical.
- SIM: visitors register with passport plus proof of entry (NCA FAQ 2026). Visitor SIM 30-day rule UNCONFIRMED.

### 2.2 Business traveller
- Business visa covers meetings, consultations and advice. Hands-on work = work permit + residence permit (Immigration Act 2000, Act 573).
- Needs employer letter + Ghanaian host invitation letter. Business visitors usually get up to 60 days.
- **Tax:** pay for work done in Ghana is taxable from day one; no minimum days. Non-resident rate flat 25%. Residence = 183+ days in any 12 months. Canada has no tax treaty; treaties in force: Belgium, Czech Republic, Denmark, France, Germany, Italy, Mauritius, Morocco, Netherlands, Qatar, Singapore, South Africa, Switzerland, UK (PwC 27.10.2026). None for China, India, Japan, Korea, Philippines, Vietnam, Malaysia, Pakistan, Bangladesh. Refer customers to a tax adviser.
- Equipment: laptops and phones are personal effects. Servers and demo kit: Red channel, temporary admission with a guarantee, re-export unchanged within 90 days. ATA Carnet not accepted. Use a licensed customs agent (GRA).
- Hours: private firms 08:00-17:00 Mon-Fri; government often closes by 15:00-16:00.
- Work permit (if staying to work): company applies; needs GIPA registration, tax clearance, audited accounts, Ghana Immigration Clinic medical, home-country police report, CV/certificates, contract, ministry support letter, Non-Citizen Ghana Card. Allow 15-20 working days minimum. GIS fees (15.05.2025): work permit US$1,293; residence US$647 (immigrant quota) or US$500 (GIPA automatic quota); dependant GH¢388. Processing time and validity: UNCONFIRMED.
- Non-Citizen Ghana Card (since 02.02.2026): US$120 first registration (paid in cedis), US$78 a year to renew. Compulsory for foreigners aged 6+ resident 90+ days a year. Portal fims.org.gh.

### 2.3 Property investor
- **Non-citizens cannot hold freehold; leasehold only, maximum 50 years at any one time** (Land Act 2020, Act 1036, s.10). An attempted freehold grant is void.
- A company with more than 40% foreign ownership is treated as non-Ghanaian for land.
- Renewal: non-Ghanaians reportedly get no automatic renewal unless the lease says so. UNCONFIRMED against the statute.
- Most land is customary (stool, skin, clan, family); no new freehold can be created over it. Register leases at the Lands Commission; use a licensed Ghanaian lawyer; verify title before paying.
- Apartment unit ownership by foreigners: legal framework UNCONFIRMED.
- Renting: Rent Act 1963 caps advance rent at 6 months (tenancies over 6 months); enforcement announced from 01.04.2026. Demands for 1-3 years upfront remain common.
- **Currency:** residents may not price or take payment for property in foreign currency; foreign-currency invoices only to expatriates or non-residents, paid into a Foreign Exchange Account (Bank of Ghana 2025/26).
- Stamp duty 1% applies on a company's stated capital (not a property rate). Property transfer taxes and ground rent: UNCONFIRMED.
- Security: burglaries in expatriate areas (Airport Residential, Cantonments, Ridge, Kokrobite).
- Buying through a company: GIPA Act 2026 (Act 1173, 15.07.2026) removed minimum capital for most foreign investors; trading enterprises US$500,000 cash equity and 75% skilled Ghanaian staff. At least 2 directors, one ordinarily resident in Ghana; qualified company secretary; ORC fees: incorporation GH¢585, annual return GH¢175; beneficial ownership at 20% threshold. Registration with GIPA after incorporation, renewed annually.
- Needs a lawyer: say so on the page. Legal content requires review by Ghanaian legal counsel.

### 2.4 Diaspora / returnee (suggested)
- Ghana recognises dual nationality; a dual nationality card shows no visa is needed (FCDO entry 04.11.2026). Check with the Ghana mission before relying on it.
- Non-Citizen Ghana Card applies to foreign passport holders without Ghanaian citizenship. Ghanaian citizens use the standard Ghana Card (not covered here; UNCONFIRMED).
- Household goods and shipping duties: UNCONFIRMED; the one cost guide found was low quality. Do not state numbers.
- Residence permit and any right-of-abode route: UNCONFIRMED; route to the mission.

### 2.5 Student (suggested)
- Student visas are outside the e-visa; embassy route applies (e-visa scope is business and tourist only, EY 06.2026). Fees and documents: UNCONFIRMED; route to the mission and the institution's admission letter.
- Health: same yellow fever, Health Declaration, malaria points. Non-Citizen Ghana Card if resident 90+ days.

### 2.6 Remote worker / digital nomad (suggested)
- **UNCONFIRMED:** no dedicated Ghana remote-work visa was found in this brief. Do not claim one exists.
- Working for a foreign employer while in Ghana on a tourist or business visa is a grey area; working without a permit is prohibited (e-visa portal). Advise a licensed immigration adviser.
- Tax: residents (183+ days) are taxed on worldwide income. Residence permit route and Non-Citizen Ghana Card as in 2.2.
- SIM, mobile money: foreigners' mobile money rules UNCONFIRMED. E-levy abolished 2025.
- Power outages: plan for UPS or power bank; 3G/4G strongest in cities.
